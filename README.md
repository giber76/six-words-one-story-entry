# Six Words, One Story

Entry for make.fun competition `u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410`.

`main.ts` writes one complete story in exactly six words to `/output/entry.txt`:

> She buried him. He kept knocking.

Six words: She / buried / him / He / kept / knocking.
A burial, then a knock from the other side — the ending inverts the
beginning, and everything in between is left for the reader.
